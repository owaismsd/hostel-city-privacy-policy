Delete Your Hostel City Account

Application: Hostel City | Developer: 2Code Studio
Last Updated: October 4, 2026

Hostel City respects your right to control your personal information. You can permanently delete your Hostel City account and associated personal data either directly from the app or by submitting a deletion request to our support team.

Account deletion is permanent Once your account deletion is completed, the deleted information cannot normally be recovered. Please make sure you want to permanently delete your account before submitting a request.

1. Delete Your Account Directly in the App

If you can access your Hostel City account, the fastest way to delete your account is through the application:

  1. Open the Hostel City app.
  2. Open your Profile.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Follow the confirmation instructions.
  6. Confirm that you want to permanently delete your account.

2. Request Account Deletion by Email

If you cannot access your account or cannot use the in-app deletion option, you can request account deletion by contacting 2Code Studio Support.

  1. Send an email from the email address associated with your Hostel City account.
  2. Use "Hostel City Account Deletion Request" as the email subject.
  3. Include the email address or phone number associated with your Hostel City account.
  4. Our support team may verify your account ownership before processing the request.

3. Data That Will Be Deleted

When your account deletion is completed, we will delete or remove personal data associated with your account from our active systems, including, where applicable:

  • Your account and authentication information.
  • Your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Your user profile and account-related information.
  • Your saved favorites.
  • Your contact history associated with the account.
  • Your reviews, ratings, comments, and problem reports.
  • Hostel listings created by your account, including associated listing information and images.
  • Other personal information directly associated with your account.

4. Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained when necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, enforce our terms, or protect the security of the service.

Where retention is required, the information will be kept only for the period necessary to fulfill the applicable legal, security, or operational requirement and will be deleted when it is no longer required.

Important: Some information that has already been publicly displayed or shared by other users may remain outside our control. For example, screenshots, copies, or information independently saved by another person cannot be removed from that person's device or external systems by Hostel City.

5. Third-Party Services

Hostel City uses third-party services such as Firebase and Cloudflare R2 to provide application functionality. Account deletion requests may require associated data to be deleted from or de-associated with these services as part of the deletion process.

Certain data may temporarily remain in backups or security logs before being automatically removed according to the applicable backup or retention cycle.

6. Processing Time

Account deletion requests submitted by email will be reviewed and processed after account ownership has been verified. We aim to process valid deletion requests within a reasonable period.

Data that must temporarily remain in backups or legally required records will be removed when the applicable retention period expires.

7. Questions About Account Deletion

If you have questions about what information will be deleted or retained, please contact us before submitting your request.
