Privacy Policy

Application: Hostel City | Developer: 2Code Studio
Last Updated: October 3, 2026

Welcome to Hostel City. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account, list a hostel, or use our services:

  • Identity Data: Full name, email address, and phone number.
  • Profile Information: Your user role (Student Seeker, Hostel Owner, or Admin) and account status.
  • Hostel Information (Owners): Property names, addresses, descriptions, room details, pricing, photos, and contact numbers.
  • Booking Information: Room types, guest counts, and check-in dates.
  • Contact History: Records when you contact hostel owners via Call or WhatsApp through the app.
  • User Content: Reviews, ratings, comments, and problem reports you submit.
  • Location Information: We process geographic coordinates for hostels. If you grant permission, we use your device's GPS location to display nearby properties on Google Maps.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To create, authenticate, and manage your secure user account.
  • To display hostel listings, facilitate bookings, and manage hostel properties.
  • To provide location-based search and distance calculations.
  • To display public reviews and ratings to help the student community.
  • To provide customer support and handle problem reports.
  • To maintain platform security, prevent fraud, and enforce developer policies.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to power core application functionality. These services process data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google): Used for Authentication, Cloud Firestore Database, and Storage.
  • Cloudflare R2: Used to securely store and serve hostel images uploaded by owners.
  • Google Maps & Play Services: Used for map display, location sorting, and credential management.

4. Data Sharing & Public Visibility

  • Between Users: When a student contacts an owner, the owner receives the contact request to facilitate the hostel booking.
  • Public Visibility: Hostel names, addresses, photos, pricing, facilities, and contact numbers are visible to app users. Reviews and ratings you post are also public.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required by applicable law or to protect platform rights.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including Firebase secure authentication, encrypted HTTPS network communications, and Firestore security rules. While we take every reasonable precaution, no transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account remains active. You can permanently delete your account at any time directly within the app settings (Profile → Settings → Delete Account). Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, favorites, contact history, reviews, and listed properties from our active databases.

7. Children's Privacy

Hostel City is intended for students and property owners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover such data, we will delete it immediately.
